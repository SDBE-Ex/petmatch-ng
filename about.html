About PetMatch

What PetMatch is

PetMatch is a free directory that helps dog and cat owners find a nearby mate for their pet, and connect directly with the other owner. No agents, no fees, no middleman.

Why we exist

Every dog and cat owner runs into the same problem eventually. You need a mate for your pet, and the only options are a random Facebook group, a friend of a friend, or pure luck. It is slow, and you rarely know who you are actually dealing with.

PetMatch gives a simpler answer. List your pet, and find real matches nearby, from real owners you can talk to directly.

Who PetMatch is for

If you have a pet worth matching, there is a place for you here:

  • Dog and cat owners looking for a mate for their pet, first time or tenth.
  • Breeders, registered or experienced, who take their work seriously.
  • Pet stores who want more customers to discover them.
  • Kennels looking to connect with nearby owners.
  • Vet clinics who want to be top of mind for the pet owners they already serve.

A friend to your pet business, not a competitor

If you run a pet store, kennel, breeding program, or vet clinic, PetMatch is not here to compete with you. We don't sell pets, we don't handle payments, and we don't take a cut of anything you arrange with another owner.

What we do is put your business in front of pet owners who are already looking for exactly what you offer. Become a PetMatch Partner, and your eligible pets show up with a Partner badge — so nearby owners find their way to you, not around you.

How it works

  • Sign in with your email — takes under a minute, no password needed.
  • Add your pet's details and a photo.
  • Your pet is discovered by nearby owners looking for a match.
  • Talk directly on WhatsApp and decide together, at your own pace.

The bigger picture

We think finding a mate for your pet should not just be easy, it should bring people together. Every match made on PetMatch connects two owners in the same community, often the same neighborhood. That means more people talking to each other, more small pet businesses getting noticed, and more responsible pet care spreading because owners are learning from each other instead of guessing alone.

We want pet care and pet life to be something worth taking pride in, not an afterthought. That starts small, one good match at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Is PetMatch a marketplace?

No. PetMatch is a directory, not a marketplace. We don't handle buying, selling, or payments of any kind — we simply help owners find each other. Anything you arrange with another owner is between the two of you.

Is PetMatch free to use?

Yes, free for everyone, always. No listing fees, no referral fees, no commissions.

Does PetMatch verify pets, owners, or businesses?

Listings are self-reported by the person who creates them. Partner applications from pet businesses are reviewed by a real person before approval, but we don't independently verify health records, vaccinations, or business registration. Always meet safely and check health and vaccination records yourself before any pairing.

How does PetMatch help pet stores, kennels, and vet clinics?

PetMatch Partners get their eligible pets shown with a Partner badge, so nearby owners discover their business while browsing for a match — a free way to get in front of people who are already looking for what you offer.
