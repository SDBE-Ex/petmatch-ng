About Petmatch

Why we exist

Every dog and cat owner runs into the same problem eventually. You need a mate for your pet, and the only options are a random Facebook group, a friend of a friend, or pure luck. It is slow, and you rarely know who you are actually dealing with.

Petmatch gives a simpler answer. List your pet, and find real matches nearby, from real owners you can talk to directly.

Petmatch helps you find a mate for your dog or cat, nearby. It's not a marketplace, we don't handle buying or selling.

Who it is for

Petmatch is for every pet owner, whether you have raised dogs your whole life or you are just trying to find a mate for your cat for the first time. It is for breeders who take their work seriously, and for pet stores and vets who want to help the customers who already trust them. If you have a pet worth matching, there is a place for you here.

The bigger picture

We think finding a mate for your pet should not just be easy, it should bring people together. Every match made on Petmatch connects two owners in the same community, often the same neighborhood. That means more people talking to each other, more small pet businesses getting noticed, and more responsible pet care spreading because owners are learning from each other instead of guessing alone.

We want pet care and pet life to be something worth taking pride in, not an afterthought. That starts small, one good match at a time.
