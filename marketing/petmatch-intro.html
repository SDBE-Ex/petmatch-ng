What it is

A free directory for pet owners, not a marketplace

PetMatch is a free directory that helps dog and cat owners connect with nearby owners, for mating, playdates, or just advice from someone who gets it. No agents, no fees, no middleman. Every listing includes a way to reach the owner directly, usually WhatsApp, and you arrange everything between yourselves.

How it works

01

Sign in with your email or Google. Takes under a minute, no password needed.

02

Add your pet's details and a photo. Species, breed, sex, age, and how to reach you.

03

Get discovered by nearby owners looking for a match, and browse listings yourself.

04

Talk directly on WhatsApp, at your own pace, and decide together what happens next.
