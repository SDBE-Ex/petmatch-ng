Partner with PetMatch

If you run a vet clinic, kennel, pet store, grooming business, or breeding program, PetMatch puts you in front of pet owners who are already looking for exactly what you offer, for free, always.

Apply to Become a Partner (It's Free)

Why partner with PetMatch?

  • Free customer discovery. Nearby owners see your business while browsing for a match. No ads, no cost.
  • A Partner badge on your listings so owners recognize you're a real, reviewed business, not a stranger.
  • A printable QR flyer linking straight to PetMatch, ready for your counter, window, or waiting room.
  • No fees, ever. No listing fees, no referral fees, no commission on anything you arrange with an owner.
  • Fast approval. A real person reviews every application, usually quickly.

Who is this for?

  • Vet clinics who want to be top of mind for the pet owners they already serve.
  • Kennels and breeding programs looking to connect with nearby owners.
  • Pet stores and groomers who want more customers to discover them.

How does it work?

  • Sign in with your email and apply. Takes a few minutes.
  • A real person reviews your application.
  • Once approved, list your eligible pets. They show up with a Partner badge.
  • Nearby owners discover your business while browsing for a match.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to become a PetMatch Partner?

No. It's free to apply and free to stay a Partner, always. No listing fees, no referral fees, no commissions on anything you arrange with an owner.

Who can apply to become a PetMatch Partner?

Vet clinics, kennels, pet stores, groomers, and breeding programs. Any pet business that wants nearby owners to discover it.

How does approval work?

Applications are reviewed by a real person, usually quickly. Once approved, your eligible pet listings show a Partner badge.

What do I actually get as a Partner?

A Partner badge on your eligible pet listings so nearby owners recognize your business while browsing for a match, plus a printable QR flyer linking to PetMatch for your counter or window.
