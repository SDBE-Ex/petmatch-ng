How PetMatch compares

There's more than one way to find a match for your dog or cat. Here's how PetMatch stacks up against the other options, honestly, tradeoffs included.

PetMatch vs. Facebook groups and classifieds

How is PetMatch different from a Facebook group or classifieds site?

Facebook groups / classifiedsPetMatch
FocusMixed in with unrelated postsBuilt only for dog and cat owners
FilteringNo filter by species, breed, or distanceFilter by species, breed, sex, and location
ContactInconsistent, often just commentsDirect contact on every listing, usually WhatsApp
CostFreeFree

Facebook groups and classifieds work, but they weren't built for this. PetMatch is: one purpose, filtered search, and a direct way to reach every listed owner.

PetMatch vs. a paid pet marketplace

How is PetMatch different from a paid pet marketplace?

Paid marketplacePetMatch
FeesListing, referral, or commission fees are commonFree for pet owners, always
PaymentsOften processes payment between buyer and sellerNever handles payments; PetMatch is a directory, not a broker
ArrangementPlatform mediates the transactionYou and the other owner arrange everything directly

If you want a platform to handle payment or mediate a transaction, PetMatch isn't built for that, and deliberately so. It's built to connect you directly with a real, nearby owner and get out of the way.

PetMatch vs. word of mouth

Is PetMatch better than word of mouth or asking around?

Asking around your existing network is often how this has always worked, and it still has a place. The limits are real: your network only reaches so far, and it can take a while to find a fit. PetMatch widens the search to owners near you that you wouldn't otherwise cross paths with, while keeping the same thing that makes word of mouth trustworthy: you're still talking to the real owner, not an agent or a listing service.

Who PetMatch is best for

  • Dog and cat owners looking for a mate, a playmate, or another owner nearby to learn from.
  • Breeders, registered or experienced, who want a focused way to connect with local owners.
  • Pet stores, kennels, and vet clinics who want to be discovered by owners already looking for what they offer.

Who it's not built for

If you want a platform to handle payment, shipping, or escrow, PetMatch isn't the fit; it doesn't do any of that on purpose. It's for owners who want to connect directly and handle the rest themselves, with the safety basics on the Trust & Safety page.
